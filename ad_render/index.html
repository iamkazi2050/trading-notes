Building a WhatsApp Bot for Your Business: What to Plan

WhatsApp is where many customers already talk to businesses, especially in India and the Gulf. A WhatsApp bot can answer common questions, take bookings and send updates without someone reading every message. The official route has some rules worth knowing before you start.

Use the official WhatsApp Business Platform

Bots should run on the WhatsApp Business Platform through Meta or an approved provider. Unofficial tools that automate a personal WhatsApp account can get the number banned.

Message templates and the 24-hour window

You can reply freely for 24 hours after a customer messages you. Outside that window, the business has to send pre-approved message templates. Plan your reminders and updates as templates and submit them early, because approval can take time.

Keep the flows short

Most good bots handle a few tasks well: answering the top questions, taking an order or booking, and passing the chat to a person when needed. Long menus frustrate users.

Connect your systems

A bot is useful when it reads real data: order status, appointment slots, account balances. Plan the API connections to your CRM, database or booking system.

Handover to a person

Always give users a way to reach a human, and show the person the full chat history when they take over.

Logs and privacy

Store conversations securely, keep only what you need, and follow the privacy rules in your country.
