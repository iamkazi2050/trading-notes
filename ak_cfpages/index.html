Starting with Algorithmic Trading in Dubai: A Practical Checklist

Interest in algorithmic trading has grown quickly in Dubai, with traders automating forex, gold, crypto and stock strategies. The first step is often a search for a ready-made bot. A better first step is to write down what you trade, where your account is held, and what you want the software to do. The rest follows from those answers.

Where is your account?

Automation runs through your broker or exchange. MT4 and MT5 brokers support Expert Advisors. Crypto exchanges offer APIs. Stock brokers vary, and some have no API for retail clients. Your account decides which tools are possible.

What should be automated?

There are three common levels. Alerts tell you when your conditions are met, and you trade by hand. Semi-automatic tools place the order after you confirm. Full automation opens and closes trades without you. Many traders start with alerts and move up once they trust the rules.

Where do the signals come from?

Your own rules, TradingView alerts, a Telegram channel or a paid signal service. Copying signals needs a copier that maps symbols and sizes to your account.

Where will it run?

An EA needs MT4 or MT5 running all the time, usually on a VPS. Choose a VPS close to your broker's server, which is often in London or New York, not in Dubai.

Time zones

Your broker's server clock is rarely Gulf time. Set trading hours in server time and check them after daylight-saving changes in Europe and the US.

Testing

Backtest on good data, then run on demo, then trade live with small sizes. Keep a log of every automated trade and compare it with what the rules should have done.
