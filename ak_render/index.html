TradingView Alerts to MT5 for Gulf Traders: Setup Notes

Many traders in the UAE and Saudi Arabia chart in TradingView and hold their accounts with MT5 brokers. Sending TradingView alerts to MT5 automatically saves them from placing every order by hand. A few details decide whether the setup works reliably.

A plan that supports webhooks

TradingView sends alerts to an external address through webhooks, which need a paid plan. Check your plan before building anything else.

Write the alert as data

Put the symbol, direction, size and stop in a short structured message, such as JSON. A bridge can read that without guessing.

Map symbols to your broker

TradingView may show XAUUSD, while your broker uses XAUUSD.m or GOLD. Add each symbol you trade to the bridge's mapping and test it.

Time zones

Your strategy may use Gulf time, TradingView may use exchange time, and the MT5 server has its own clock. Keep any time filters in one time zone and check them after daylight-saving changes in Europe and the US.

Duplicate alerts

Alerts can fire twice in fast markets. The bridge should ignore a repeat within a short window.

Test on demo

Run the setup on a demo account for a week and compare each alert with the trade that opened in MT5.
