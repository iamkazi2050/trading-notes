Offering Trade Copying to Your Clients: Notes for Brokers

Brokers and introducing brokers often get the same request from clients: "Can I copy my mentor's trades into my account?" Building a copy service in-house takes months. A white-label or API-based trade copier can get a broker there faster, but the choice needs care.

Decide who the copier is for

Is it for clients following a signal provider, money managers running several client accounts, or your own dealing desk? Each case needs different controls over who can copy whom.

Account types and platforms

List the platforms your clients use, such as MT4, MT5 or your own trading platform. A copier has to place trades on all of them, with the right symbol names and contract sizes for your server.

Risk controls

Clients should be able to set their own size rule, a maximum lot size and a loss limit. The broker side should be able to pause a provider whose trades look wrong.

Branding and onboarding

If the copier carries your brand, check how clients sign up, connect their accounts and see their copied trades. A confusing onboarding loses more clients than a missing feature.

Logs for support

Your support team will be asked why a trade did not copy. Each signal and each account's result should be visible in a log they can search.

Compliance

Copy trading may count as a regulated activity in some countries. Check the rules with your regulator before launch.
